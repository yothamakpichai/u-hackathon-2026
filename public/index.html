One town. A building for every money skill.

Everything you do, in any building, feeds one daily streak. Five minutes a day becomes a habit.

NO. 01

School

Short lessons, market missions and checkpoints for three age levels, read aloud, with XP, a daily goal and a streak.

NO. 02

Market Lab

A crypto market that changes daily, with real chart tools and real traps. The Coach takes your hand, and the Arena puts you in a live market with players and bots.

NO. 03

Future You

Five hundred projections of your own plan in today’s baht. Your character ages in front of you, and you can talk to them.

NO. 04

Money Town

A Piggy Bank for your real savings, a cow you earn, an orchard that rewards waiting, a store, and a bank with credit and the quick-cash trap.

NO. 05 · IN RESEARCH

Research Lab

Stocks and gold come next. We study them before we build them; for now the lab teaches one real lesson, like how Thai bar gold is priced.
