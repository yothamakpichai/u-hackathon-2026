Raising the generation nobody can fool.

Penny is a money companion for life. Kids from 8 and grown-ups practise saving, borrowing, investing and spotting scams in a living town, and Penny explains every step.

Play Penny Judge demo

Hi, I’m Penny. Ask me anything about money. I’ll explain it plainly, and I’ll never try to sell you anything.

Penny teaches with simulated money, never recommends a coin, stock or trade, and never holds your money. Please don’t share personal details.

Money habits are largely set by about age seven.1 Thai households owe 85.9% of GDP.2 A Thai woman can expect to reach 80, a man 72, and both will need to fund the years after work.3 Nobody gets a practice round. Penny is one.

One town. Every money skill has a building.

Whatever you do in any building feeds one daily streak, so five minutes a day becomes a habit.

01

School

Short lessons, market missions and checkpoints for three age levels, all read aloud. XP, a daily goal and a streak keep you coming back.

02

Market Lab

A crypto market that changes every day, with real chart tools and real traps: whale dumps, rug pulls, wash-trading bots. The Coach takes your hand through every step, and the Arena puts you in a live market with other players and bots.

03

Future You

Five hundred projections of your own plan in today’s baht, with Thai inflation and currency scenarios. Your character ages in front of you, and you can talk to them.

04

Money Town

A Piggy Bank for your real savings, a cow you earn by doing, an orchard that rewards waiting, a store, and a bank where you meet interest, credit scores and the quick-cash trap before real life does.

05

Research Lab

Stocks and gold are next. We are studying them before we build them; until then the lab teaches one real lesson, like how Thai bar gold is priced.

Practise in a simulation. Change real habits.
