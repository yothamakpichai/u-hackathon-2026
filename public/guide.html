User guide

How to play Penny

Everything you need in about five minutes: your first visit, every building in the town, and how to stay safe. Penny uses simulated money, except the Piggy Bank, which only records the real savings you choose to write down.

Your first visit

  1. Pick your birth year. The cake lights one candle per year and sets your level: Explorer (8–12), Trader (13–15) or Builder (16+). Grown-ups can choose any level. Your age is self-declared and stays on your device.
  2. Make your character. Choose skin tone, hair, outfit and glasses, or tap “Surprise me”. There is no gender question. Future You will show this character growing older.
  3. Take the Money Check. Fifteen short questions, the same for everyone, each with a Listen button. You get a Money Health Score out of 100 and one of four Money Types. In a hurry? Tap “Take it later”.
  4. Enter your town. Every building is one money skill. Anything you do in any building counts toward your daily goal and your streak.

Judging Penny? Tap Judge demo on the home page to load a clearly labelled demo profile with a full town, so nothing is waiting on a timer.

The town

The town is your home screen. At the top you see your streak, today’s XP and your Money Health Score. Tap a building to go in, and tap ⓘ on any building to read why it works and the research behind it.

School

The Learning Path has three units per level. Each unit has lessons, a market mission you complete in the Market Lab, and a checkpoint quiz you can retry freely. Everything you do earns XP toward a daily goal of 30 XP, and doing something every day keeps your streak alive.

  • Lessons are short cards with a quiz; tap Listen to hear them.
  • Missions read your real numbers from the market you just played.
  • The Learning partners shelf is where partner academies will add extra lessons.

Market Lab

Today’s market is the same for everyone on the same day. You start with practice money and trade the town’s coins while news, hype and scams play out.

Reading the chart

Each candle shows one period: where the price opened and closed, and the highest and lowest it reached. Green means it closed higher, red lower. Volume bars show how much was traded. Every tool has an ⓘ that says what it shows and what it cannot tell you.

Trading

  • Market order: buy or sell now. Penny shows how much your trade will move the price before you confirm.
  • Limit order: buy or sell only at the price you choose.
  • Stop-loss: sell automatically if the price falls to a level, to limit a loss. In a crash it can fill lower than you set.

Basic or Advanced. Basic is a simple swap card: pick a coin, enter an amount, check the impact, confirm. Advanced is a full exchange view with a watchlist, several charts, indicators, drawing tools, an order book, and tabs for positions, open orders and history.

The Coach

The Coach pauses the market and walks you through it step by step: reading a candle, volume and indicators, your first trade, and surviving a whale dump played twice, once panic-selling and once holding.

Create a coin and the explorer

Builders can launch their own coin and watch what hype, unlocks and a copycat rug pull do to it. Every trade is a transaction sealed into blocks on a simulated blockchain, which you can inspect in the block explorer and even try to tamper with.

Arena

The Arena is a live market shared by real players and named bots. You need an account to trade. Kids unlock it with a Money Health Score of 70; grown-ups can enter directly. Anyone can watch without an account, players can launch their own coins, and a daily season awards medals.

Future You

Enter your age, how much you can save each month and the age you want to use it. Penny runs 500 projections and shows the middle one and the range where 8 out of 10 land, always in today’s baht so rising prices are already counted. Try Standard or Pro for more inputs, inflation and currency scenarios, and the 1997, 2020 and 2022 shocks.

It is a projection, not a prediction. Nobody knows the future; the range shows what could happen.

Talk to Future You. Chat with your future self about your plan, and hear the answer in the voice you choose. You can also export a report to save as a PDF.

Money Town

  • Piggy Bank. Set goals and write down the real cash you save or spend. Penny never touches your money. Rewards come from habits such as logging and planning, never from amounts. Try the date challenge or the birthday-year challenge.
  • Farm and Orchard. You cannot buy a cow; you earn one by doing things around town. A grown cow gives milk once a day. In the orchard, a sprout is ready in minutes, a big tree takes days and gives far more.
  • Store. Buy, hold and sell goods whose prices move; some spoil if you hold them too long.
  • Bank. Borrow with interest and see the total cost before you agree. The Town Card shows what paying only the minimum really costs. Your credit score changes your next rate. Beware Quick Cash.

Research Lab

Stocks and gold are still being researched. Tap the lab for a real lesson card, such as how Thai bar gold is priced.

Ask Penny

Tap Ask Penny anywhere to ask a question in English. Penny explains ideas, reviews your last market and explains your Money Check. Penny teaches and never tells you what to buy. Please don’t share personal details such as your full name, school or phone number.

Accounts and parents

You can play everything as a guest. An optional account (username, password and birth year) saves your progress across devices and unlocks the Arena. Accounts for players under 13 stay inactive until a parent approves them.

From your account, show the parent link or QR code to a grown-up. On their own phone they can see your progress, confirm your Piggy Bank entries and reset a forgotten password.

Safety

  • Everything is simulated except the Piggy Bank record you write yourself.
  • No ads or sponsor banners for anyone under 20.
  • No analytics, and only one cookie when you use an account.
  • Real trading only happens on licensed exchanges with identity checks, and only for adults. Penny never handles real money.

Questions

Is this real crypto?

No. Every coin, price and blockchain in Penny is simulated for learning.

Why does my progress disappear on another phone?

Guest progress lives on one device. Create an account to move it.

Can I play in Thai?

Penny is English-only for now.
